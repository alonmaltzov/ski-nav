Ski Nav privacy

Ski Nav is a personal ski trip app for a small group of friends. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

Location

Your location is used on your phone to show you on the piste map and track your runs. Your ski history stays on your phone.

Group sharing

Only if you join a trip group, your latest position and name are shared with the people in that group, during trip days until 6 pm. They are stored on our group server (Supabase, EU) and deleted when the trip is deleted. You can stop sharing in Settings at any time.

Voice and microphone

The microphone is used only while you tap the guide's talk button. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone and is not sent anywhere.

Health (Apple Watch)

The Watch app saves each ski day as a workout in Apple Health. Health data is not shared with anyone.
